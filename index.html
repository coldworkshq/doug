How it works

Sniff. Fetch. Remember.

01 — SNIFF

Score every PR, deterministically

Hotspots learned from your repo's own defect history plus structural signals. No model in the hot path. Nothing leaves your repo.

02 — FETCH

Bring a human to the right PR

The risky slice gets routed with evidence attached; the rest is cleared with a receipt. Like any good Saint Bernard: find the buried one, bring help.

03 — REMEMBER

Join every verdict to its outcome

Revert, hotfix, rollback, or quiet survival — measured at 14 days. Doug's calibration is public, so trust is earned, not asserted.
